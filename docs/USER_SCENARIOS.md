# MIZAN — User Scenarios

Narrative scenarios grounding the requirements in realistic usage.

## Scenario 1 — Salary Day
Layla, 29, works in Dubai and gets paid on the 26th of each month. On payday she opens MIZAN, taps Add → Income, logs her salary. The dashboard updates instantly: available balance rises, and MIZAN's AI assistant surfaces a proactive note: "You have 3 bills due before your next payday totaling AED 2,150 — your remaining budget after bills is AED X." She reviews her budgets for the month and adjusts her dining-out limit slightly down after seeing last month's overspend.

## Scenario 2 — Impulse Purchase Check
Omar is considering a AED 1,200 purchase. He opens the AI assistant and asks "Can I afford this?" The assistant retrieves his current budget status and upcoming bills (via agent tools, not guesses), and responds with a grounded answer referencing his actual remaining discretionary budget for the month, not a generic answer.

## Scenario 3 — Missed Bill Prevention
Fatima previously missed a credit card payment because she tracked bills in her head. In MIZAN she adds her recurring bills once with due dates. She receives a notification 3 days before each due date and again on the due date. The dashboard also surfaces upcoming bills in the next 7 days.

## Scenario 4 — New User Onboarding
Khalid downloads MIZAN, creates an account, and is guided to add his primary income and 2–3 recurring bills before reaching the dashboard, so the dashboard is meaningful on first real use rather than empty.

## Scenario 5 — Saving for a Goal
Mariam wants to save AED 20,000 for a wedding within 12 months. She creates a Financial Goal, and MIZAN suggests a monthly contribution target based on the timeline. Each month she logs a savings contribution and watches the goal progress bar move.

## Scenario 6 — Arabic-First User
Ahmad prefers using his phone entirely in Arabic. He switches MIZAN's language to Arabic in Settings; the entire app — including the AI assistant's replies, number formatting, and layout direction — mirrors to RTL and reads naturally, not like a translated English app.

## Scenario 7 — Reviewing Spending Patterns
At month end, Noor opens the AI assistant and asks "Why did my expenses increase this month?" The assistant analyzes category-level deltas versus the prior month and responds with the top 2–3 drivers, grounded in her actual transactions.

## Scenario 8 — Switching Themes at Night
Yousef uses MIZAN before bed and finds the light theme too bright. He switches to Dark Mode from Settings (or lets System Default follow his OS setting); the switch persists and every screen — charts included — remains legible.
